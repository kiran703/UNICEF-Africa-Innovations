# UNICEF Africa Innovations Portfolio 2026
Open-Source Solutions for Africa | Aligned with UNICEF Venture Fund

This portfolio presents 3 frontier-tech prototypes designed for youth in Global South, fully open-source and offline-first.

### 1. AfyaHealth - Kenya 🇰🇪
**Problem:** Lack of doctors in rural Kenya.
**Solution:** AI health assistant that checks symptoms offline in Swahili & English.
**Tech:** Flutter, TensorFlow Lite, Firebase

### 2. EduConnect - Uganda 🇺🇬
**Problem:** Students have no internet for learning.
**Solution:** Offline learning app with voice lessons in local languages.
**Tech:** Flutter, Firebase

### 3. FarmNaija - Nigeria 🇳🇬
**Problem:** Young farmers lack weather, disease, and market info.
**Solution:** AI Crop Doctor + Weather + Market Price in Hausa/Yoruba/Igbo.
**Tech:** Flutter, TensorFlow Lite

**License:** MIT (Open Source)
**Built for:** UNICEF Venture Fund Internship Application 2026
